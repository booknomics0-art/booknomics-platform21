**Important editorial limitations:** These checks confirm database persistence, formatting and that excerpts exactly occur in Booknomics' stored summaries. They **do not** establish correctness against the original books, independent verification of premises, plausible distractors in every case, or true mastery of advanced reasoning. Many questions use common stem structures and compare multiple ideas; independent book-by-book human-quality editing is still required. Source pages themselves may have generic or inaccurate language. Do not call these 500 book sets independently editorial-approved. `editorial_review_required: true` is recorded per generated question.

**Visibility caution:** At audit time `book_assets` has a public SELECT policy without an asset-status filter, and the reader's `useBookAssets` fetches `quiz_data` without filtering on `status`. Consequently `draft` does not mean hidden from site visitors; an authenticated browser test and editorial publication gate should be performed separately. No global RLS policy was modified during this batch.

**Safety of writes:** Only records with `status='draft'` and an empty `quiz_data` array were selected for insertion. Existing sets were not touched. After initial persistence, question text was disambiguated with title (and author where needed), and all 5,000 questions were rechecked.
