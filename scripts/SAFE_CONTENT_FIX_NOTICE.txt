Booknomics content safety notice

Legacy fix-phase scripts that auto-expand book summaries with generic prose are disabled.
They must not be used to reach word-count or quality thresholds.

Required workflow for missing/short content:
1. Audit the book and identify the exact missing section.
2. Use book-specific, source-verified facts, themes, examples and analysis.
3. Do not invent literary influence, translations, awards, historical context, structure, symbolism, philosophy, reception or character traits.
4. Re-run content-quality checks before publishing/indexing.

The production database also blocks known repeated/generic template content from becoming indexable.
